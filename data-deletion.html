Delete Your Data — Grava

Last updated: June 2026

Grava (package com.grava.app) lets you delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Delete from inside the app (instant)

  1. Open Grava and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → About & legal (the About screen).
  3. Tap Delete account & data and confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your account (email + login), your ratings and comments from our servers, and all data stored on your device.

Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app, email gravaapp@gmail.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my account." We will delete your account and associated data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and login credentials).
  • All comments and ratings you posted, and the reports you filed.
  • All on-device data: library, favorites, progress, sessions, profile, and block list.

What is retained

Nothing that personally identifies you. We do not store your email, name, password, or precise location. Uninstalling the app also removes all on-device data.
