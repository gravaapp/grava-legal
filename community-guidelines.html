Grava — Community Guidelines

Last updated: September 2026

Grava is a place to share what you play. These rules cover moments, comments, groups and profiles. You accept them in the app before you first post, comment or create a group. Breaking them can get your content removed or your account banned.

Be kind

No harassment, bullying, threats or hate — attacking people for who they are is never okay. Don’t share anyone’s private information (real name, address, phone, photos) without their permission.

Keep it safe for everyone

No nudity or sexual content. No real-world violence or gore (violence inside a game, shown as part of the game, is fine). Nothing that encourages self-harm. Nothing illegal — including selling drugs, weapons or accounts.

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

We never allow content or behaviour that sexualises, grooms, exploits or endangers anyone under 18 — including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). When we find it we remove it, keep what the law requires as evidence, report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and the relevant authorities, and permanently ban the accounts involved. Report it with “Child safety”: the moment is hidden straight away and reviewed first. See our Child Safety Standards. If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police.

Be real

No spam, scams or engagement bait (“like if…”). No fake accounts, impersonation, or bought likes and follows. Say when something is an ad or a sponsored post.

Share what’s yours

Post screenshots and moments you captured yourself or have the right to share. No leaks, pirated games, or footage shared before its release date. Rights holders can report copyright infringement to us.

Mind the story

Tag the story point when a moment is about the plot — players who haven’t got there see it blurred. Don’t slip spoilers into comments, group names or captions of untagged moments.

Age

You must be at least 13 to post, comment or join groups (older where your local law requires it).

What happens when something breaks the rules

Anyone can report a moment or block a player. A child-safety report hides a moment at once; otherwise a moment is hidden after several reports while we review it. If it’s yours, you’ll see why in the app and can appeal — a person reviews appeals within 7 days. Serious or repeated breaches lead to a permanent ban.

Contact

Questions, appeals or reports that can’t wait: gravaapp@gmail.com.
