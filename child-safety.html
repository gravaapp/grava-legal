Grava — Child Safety Standards

Last updated: September 2026

These are the child safety standards for Grava (Android app com.grava.app on Google Play), an independent game-tracking app with a community where players share short posts called “moments”, comment, follow each other and join groups.

Zero tolerance. Grava prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. We remove it, report it to the authorities, and permanently ban the accounts involved.

What is prohibited

On Grava you may never post, share, request or link to:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, real or computer-generated.
  • Content that sexualises anyone under 18, including drawings, “jokes” and role-play.
  • Grooming — building a relationship with a minor for sexual purposes, or trying to move a conversation with a minor somewhere private.
  • Sextortion, or threats to share intimate images.
  • Trafficking, or any content that endangers the safety of a child.

How to report

  1. In the app: tap ⋯ on any moment → Report → Child safety. The moment is hidden from everyone immediately and reviewed first. You can also block the player.
  2. By email: gravaapp@gmail.com with the subject “Child safety”. Please describe where you saw it; don’t attach or forward the material itself.
  3. If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency services first. You can also report CSAM directly to the NCMEC CyberTipline.

What we do

  • A child-safety report hides the moment for everyone straight away, before any review.
  • We review child-safety reports ahead of every other report.
  • When we confirm CSAM or other CSAE, we remove it, keep only what the law requires as evidence, report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to the relevant national authorities, and permanently ban the accounts involved.
  • We cooperate with law-enforcement requests made through lawful channels.

How Grava is built to protect young people

  • You must be at least 13 to use Grava’s community features.
  • Everyone must accept our Community Guidelines, which include these standards, before they can post, comment or create a group.
  • Grava has no private messaging. Everything shared goes to an audience the poster chose — everyone, their followers, or a group — and can be reported by anyone who sees it.
  • Any player can block another, and blocked players disappear from every feed.

Compliance

Grava complies with applicable child safety laws, including the obligation to report CSAM to NCMEC or the relevant regional authority.

Child safety contact

For questions about Grava’s CSAE prevention practices, contact gravaapp@gmail.com.
